## Page layout
- Dream Arena opponent selection uses the shared AppSidebar and DashboardHeader; cinematic loading and battles remain full-screen to preserve the match experience.
- On player selection, a live Polar trial with exactly one linked tag and no recorded trial AI usage opens that player once per account (localStorage flag); otherwise the user picks.- Subscription access is decided only by `supabase/functions/_shared/subscription-access.ts` (used by get-subscription-status and ai-access); cancellation uses Polar `cancel_at_period_end`, never DELETE, so users keep what they were promised until the period end.
